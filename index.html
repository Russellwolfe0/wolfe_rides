A trip plan built around how you ride.

Long-distance motorcycle travel goes wrong in small ways: a fuel gap you didn’t see coming, a border that closes early, a “shortcut” that turns into 80 miles of sand. A.J. has hit those problems so you don’t have to.

Each plan is written for you: your bike, your experience, how many days you have, and how rough you want the road to be. You get the route, the stops, and the notes that only come from having been there.

How a custom plan works

  1. Tell A.J. about the trip

    Where you want to ride, your bike, your dates and your experience.

  2. Talk it through

    A short call or email thread to settle the route style and pace.

  3. Get your plan

    Day-by-day route, fuel and lodging stops, and gear and paperwork notes.

  4. Ride it

    Questions on the road? Ask A.J. before you leave.
